# Valbuena Lab

## Structural Biophysics and Molecular Engineering

Understanding how molecular structure, conformational fluctuations and mechanical properties shape biological function.

---

### About

We investigate how molecular structure, equilibrium conformational dynamics and mechanical properties determine the function of biological assemblies. Combining atomic force microscopy (AFM), single-molecule biophysics, molecular dynamics simulations and structural biology, we study protein-based systems to uncover the principles governing self-assembly, structural transitions and biological activity, from physical virology and molecular engineering to protein misfolding, amyloids and TDP-43 aggregation.

---

### Research Areas

- Structural Biophysics
- Physical Virology
- Single-Molecule Biophysics
- Molecular Dynamics Simulations
- Molecular Engineering
- Protein Self-Assembly and Aggregation
